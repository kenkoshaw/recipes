# Butter Rolls

**Makes** 18 rolls · **Total** 2 hr 45 · **Active** 40 min

Soft Japanese-style enriched rolls, scaled up to use a full 7g yeast packet. The original makes 8 (multiply everything by 0.43).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Unsalted butter, softened | 58 |
| Egg wash (1 egg + 1 tbsp milk + pinch of salt) | ~20 |

### Dough bowl (step 1)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Strong white bread flour | 467 |
| Caster sugar | 47 |
| Salt, on the opposite side of the bowl from the yeast | 7 |
| Fast-action dried yeast | 7 |
| Egg | 1 large (58g) |
| Water | 152 |
| Milk | 117 |

## Method

1. **Mix the dough** · 5 min

   Mix the dough bowl until you have a shaggy dough.

2. **Knead, then add butter** · 15 min

   Knead for about 5 minutes until the dough comes together. Work in the softened butter (**58g**) a little at a time, then keep kneading for 8–10 minutes until the dough is smooth, elastic and passes the windowpane test.

3. **First proof** · 60 min

   Cover and leave somewhere warm until the dough has doubled. Check it by poking a floured finger in: the hole should stay put.

4. **Divide and rest** · 15 min

   Knock the dough back and weigh it (about **910g** total). Divide it into 18 pieces of about **50g** each, roll each one into a ball, then cover and rest for 10–15 minutes.

5. **Shape** · 15 min

   Roll each ball into a teardrop, then flatten it with a rolling pin into a long triangle. Roll it up from the wide end towards the point. Place the rolls seam-side down on two lined trays.

6. **Second proof** · 40 min

   Cover loosely and leave until the rolls are about 1.5× their size and puffy. Preheat the oven to 180°C fan towards the end of the proof.

7. **Egg wash and bake** · 12 min

   Brush the rolls thinly with egg wash (**~20g**) and bake for 10–12 minutes, until they're deep golden and 88–90°C inside. Cool on a rack.

## Tips

- 強力粉 = strong white bread flour; ドライイースト = fast-action dried yeast.
- Use caster sugar, as it's the closest UK match to Japanese 上白糖. Granulated also works.
- Use unsalted butter. If you only have salted, cut the salt to about **6g**.
- If you add too much egg, cut back the water (egg is about 75% water) or add flour 1 tbsp at a time until the dough is tacky but not sticky.
- Egg wash: strain it or beat it well, and brush it on thinly just before baking. Yolk plus milk gives a darker, shinier finish.
- To freeze unbaked, freeze straight after shaping (before the second proof). Thaw overnight in the fridge, then proof for 1–2 hrs, egg wash and bake. Freezing after the second proof risks the rolls deflating.
- More reliable freezing: par-bake the rolls until set but pale (about two-thirds of the bake time), cool and freeze. To serve, egg wash and finish in the oven for 5–8 minutes.
