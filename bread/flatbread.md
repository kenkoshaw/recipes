# Flatbread

No-yeast flatbreads cooked in a dry pan. Soft is a milk-and-butter dough with a 30 min rest. Yoghurt is quicker, with no rest, and uses Greek yoghurt and bicarb.

## Shopping List (Soft)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Plain flour | 350g |
| Whole milk | 200ml |
| Unsalted butter | 50g |

## Ingredients (Soft)

**Makes** 6 flatbreads, about 20cm · **Total** 55 min · **Active** 25 min

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Plain flour (for dusting and adjusting the dough) | ¼ cup (~35) |

### Milk and butter jug (step 1)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Milk | 185ml (~190) |
| Unsalted butter, cubed | 50 |

### Mixer bowl (step 2)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Plain flour | 2 cups (~300) |
| Salt | ½ tsp (3) |

## Method (Soft)

1. **Melt the butter into the milk** · 1 min

   Microwave the milk and butter jug in 30-second bursts until the butter has just melted. It should be warm, not hot.

2. **Mix and knead** · 5 min

   Fit the mixer bowl to the Kenwood with the dough hook. Pour in the milk and butter jug and mix on low until it comes together, then knead on medium-low for 3 min until smooth.

   If it's sticky, add dusting flour (**~35g**) 1 tbsp at a time until it's soft and no longer sticks to your fingers.

3. **Rest** · 30 min

   Cover the dough, or wrap it in cling film, and leave it at room temperature for 30 min.

4. **Divide and roll** · 5 min

   Weigh the dough (about **540g**) and divide it into 6 pieces of about **90g**. Roll each into a ball, then roll it out on a floured surface to a 20cm round, 2–3mm thick.

5. **Cook** · 15 min

   Heat a non-stick frying pan over high heat with no oil. Cook each flatbread for 1–1½ min, until it puffs up and has golden spots underneath. Flip and cook the other side for 1 min.

   Stack the cooked flatbreads wrapped in a clean tea towel, so they steam and stay soft.

## Tips (Soft)

- To make ahead, keep the dough in the fridge for up to 3 days. Separate rolled-out rounds with baking paper or cling film, not flour.
- Cooked flatbreads freeze well.
- Wholemeal flour works in place of plain.

## Shopping List (Yoghurt)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Plain flour | 350g |
| Greek yoghurt | 300g |
| Bicarbonate of soda | 3.5g |
| Lemons | 1 |

## Ingredients (Yoghurt)

**Makes** 6 flatbreads, about 20cm · **Total** 25 min · **Active** 25 min

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Plain flour (for dusting) | ~20 |

### Mixer bowl (step 1)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Plain flour | 300 |
| Bicarbonate of soda | ¾ tsp (3.5) |
| Salt | ½ tsp (3) |

### Dough yoghurt bowl (step 1)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Greek yoghurt | 300 |
| Lemon juice | 1 tbsp (15) |

## Method (Yoghurt)

1. **Mix the dough** · 3 min

   Fit the mixer bowl to the Kenwood with the dough hook, tip in the dough yoghurt bowl and mix on low for 1 min, then medium-low for 1 min, until a soft dough forms.

   Work quickly from here: the bicarb starts reacting as soon as the yoghurt goes in. If the dough is very sticky, add dusting flour (**~20g**) 1 tbsp at a time.

2. **Divide and roll** · 5 min

   Weigh the dough (about **620g**) and divide it into 6 pieces of about **100g**. Roll each out on a floured surface to a 20cm round, 2–3mm thick.

3. **Cook the flatbreads** · 18 min

   Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat with no oil. Cook each flatbread for 1½ min a side, until puffed with golden spots.

   Stack them wrapped in a clean tea towel so they stay soft.

## Tips (Yoghurt)

- The yoghurt and lemon juice are the acid for the bicarb, so use full-fat Greek or Greek-style yoghurt, not a thin or sweetened one.

## Tips

- Higher heat and thinner rounds give a crispier crust that can crack when rolled. Brush with olive oil or melted butter to soften it again, and keep the flatbreads in the tea towel.
