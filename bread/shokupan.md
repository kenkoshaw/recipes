# Shokupan

**Makes** 1 square loaf (10×10.5×20.5cm lidded tin, ~10 slices) · **Total** 2 hr 45 + yudane overnight · **Active** ~45 min

Soft, fluffy Japanese milk bread made with the yudane method: a scalded flour paste made the night before keeps the crumb moist. Three rolls of dough sit side by side in a lidded loaf tin, baked with the lid on for a square loaf, and the Kenwood dough hook does the kneading. The milk powder, yeast-first mixing and lid-on rise height come from [Just One Cookbook's shokupan](https://www.justonecookbook.com/japanese-milk-bread-shokupan/).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Bread flour, about 12% protein (yudane) | 62 |
| Boiling water, above 90°C (yudane) | 50ml (50g) |
| Water, room temperature (cold in a warm kitchen) | 170ml (170g) |
| Sugar | 1¼ tbsp (19g) |
| Instant dry yeast | 1 sachet, 2¼ tsp (7g) |

### Mixer bowl (step 2)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Bread flour, about 12% protein | 250 |
| Salt | 1¼ tsp (6g) |
| Skim milk powder | 2½ tbsp (17g) |
| Unsalted butter, room temperature | 4 tsp (19g) |

## Method

1. **Make the yudane the night before** · 5 min + overnight

   Put the bread flour (**62g**) in a bowl, pour in 50ml (**50g**) boiling water, above 90°C, and mix well. Cover with cling film and refrigerate overnight.

2. **Mix the yeast, then the dough** · 5 min

   In a jug, whisk the water (**170g**) with the sugar (**19g**) until dissolved, then whisk in the yeast (**7g**).

   Tear the yudane (**112g** in total) into small pieces into the mixer bowl. Make a well in the middle and pour in the yeast water. Fit the dough hook and mix on low until everything is combined.

3. **Knead** · 20 min

   Once everything is combined, turn the Kenwood up to medium-low and knead for 20 min.

   The dough should be 26–28°C straight after kneading. If it's warmer, use colder water next time.

4. **First rise** · 35–60 min

   Roll the dough into a ball and put it in a lightly greased bowl. Cover with cling film and leave at about 30°C for 35–60 min, until doubled in size.

   To check, dust a finger with flour and poke the dough. If the hole stays and the dough doesn't bounce back, it's ready.

5. **Divide and rest** · 25 min

   Punch the dough down and cut it into three equal pieces with a scraper (about **199g** each, from the **597g** total of the ingredients). Roll each into a ball, cover with a wet cloth and rest for 20 min.

6. **Roll out and shape** · 15 min

   Roll each piece with a rolling pin into a rectangle of about 12×18cm. Fold each tightly towards the centre from the left and right, so it's about 6cm wide, without letting any air in. Turn it 90 degrees and roll it up from one end.

   Lightly spray the 10×10.5×20.5cm tin and the inside of its lid. Put the three rolls in side by side, seam down and spirals facing the long sides of the tin: one at each end and one in the middle.

7. **Second rise** · 30–60 min

   Cover the tin with a wet cloth and leave until the dough reaches 75–80% of the tin's height, about 2–2.5cm below the rim.

   The gap leaves room for oven spring. If the dough rises higher, the lid may not close, or the dough may overflow and stick.

   Slide the lid on. Heat the oven to 190°C while the dough rises.

8. **Bake with the lid on** · 30–35 min

   Bake at 190°C with the lid on for 30–35 min. Slide the lid off to check: the top should be golden and the loaf should sound hollow when tapped on the base.

9. **Cool** · 1 hr+

   Take the loaf out of the tin straight away and cool it on a wire rack. Slice it the next day if you can; it's so soft that it tears when still warm.

## Tips

- **Kenwood use**: the dough hook mixes and kneads (steps 2–3). The source's mixer speeds (1, then 5 or 6) are for its own mixer, not a Kenwood, so these steps say "low" and "medium-low" instead. I haven't checked how long or how fast your Kenwood manual allows the dough hook to run, so look before kneading for the full 20 min.
- **Flour**: choose a bread flour with around 12% protein (from the source).
- **Yeast**: **35g** of fresh yeast can replace the **7g** of instant dry yeast (5× the dry weight, as in the source).
- **Milk powder**: skim milk powder and water replace the fresh milk, as in Just One Cookbook's shokupan. For fresh milk instead, use 190ml (**194g**) whole milk in place of the water and powder, and cut the butter to **12g**.
- **Yudane timing**: best made the night before, but it can be made and rested for a shorter time (from the source).
- **Lid off**: for a domed loaf, leave the lid off and let the dough rise just above the rim instead. Brush with a little beaten egg, then bake at 185°C for 25–30 min, as in the source.
- **Tin**: the source uses a 10×20×9cm tin (~1,800cm³) and ~478g of dough. Your lidded tin holds ~2,150cm³, so the recipe is scaled 1.25× to ~600g of dough to fill it once the lid is on.
