# Crêpes

**Makes** 8 crêpes (4 servings) · **Total** 50 min · **Active** 20 min

Thin crêpes built from double cream diluted with water instead of milk. The batter is the recipe; steps 6–8 are optional toppings, pick one or none.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Eggs | 2 medium (100g) |
| Double cream | 75 |
| Water | 225 |
| Butter, for the pan | 20 |
| Caster sugar — optional topping | 30 |
| Lemon juice — optional topping | 1 lemon (40g) |
| Double cream, to whip — optional topping | 150 |
| Golden syrup — optional topping | 100 |
| Water, for the syrup — optional topping | 25 |

### Dry mix bowl (step 2)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Plain flour | 100 |
| Salt | 1 |

### Cream flavouring bowl (step 7)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Icing sugar — optional topping | 10 |
| Vanilla bean extract — optional topping | 1 |

## Method

1. **Thin the cream** · 2 min

   Whisk the double cream (**75g**) into the water (**225g**) in a jug until uniform. This lands at roughly 6% fat, standing in for 300g of milk. Combining them first prevents lumps later. Put a small cup on the scale, zero it, and pour **50g** of the mixture into it to hold back for step 4. If whipped cream is going on top, do step 7 before this — diluted cream will not whip, and it is easier to keep the two lots of cream separate from the start.

2. **Make the batter** · 5 min

   Whisk the dry mix bowl together, make a well, and beat in 2 medium eggs (**100g**). Add the rest of the cream-and-water mixture in three stages, whisking smooth between each. Cue: single-cream consistency — coats the back of a spoon, then runs straight off.

3. **Rest the batter** · 30 min

   Cover and leave at room temperature. The flour swells and the batter thickens noticeably.

4. **Recheck and loosen** · 1 min

   Stir, then add the held-back cup (**50g**) a splash at a time until it flows again. Too thin is easier to correct than too thick.

5. **Cook the crêpes** · 15 min

   Heat a wide non-stick pan over medium. Wipe with butter (**20g**) between crêpes — a little on kitchen paper, not a pool. Ladle roughly **50g** of batter per crêpe and tilt immediately; it should reach the edge in about 2 seconds. Cook 45–60 seconds until the edges lift and the underside is lacy brown, flip, then 20–30 seconds on the second side.

6. **Optional — sugar and lemon** · 2 min

   Scatter caster sugar (**30g**) over the hot crêpe first so it melts into the butter, then the juice of 1 lemon (**40g**), then roll. Fresh lemon only.

7. **Optional — whipped cream** · 5 min

   Chill the bowl and whisk for 15 minutes in the freezer. Whip double cream (**150g**) on medium speed, adding the cream flavouring bowl once it thickens. Stop at soft peaks — the peak flops over when the whisk is lifted. Chill until serving.

8. **Optional — loosened golden syrup** · 2 min

   Warm golden syrup (**100g**) gently, then stir in water (**25g**) until uniform. Warming alone gets close to maple pourability; the water takes it the rest of the way. Cold syrup and water will not combine.

## Tips

- Caster sugar over granulated for the topping — it dissolves in the residual heat and juice for a glaze rather than a gritty crunch. Granulated works if that texture is wanted.
- Icing sugar in the whipped cream, not caster: it dissolves instantly and its cornflour adds stability.
- Whip on medium, not high. Smaller bubbles, more stable, and more warning before it splits. Finish the last 20% by hand.
- Overwhipped cream can be rescued by folding in a splash of unwhipped cream and whisking gently by hand, unless it has gone fully buttery.
- Vanilla is optional even within the optional cream — if the lemon topping is on, it gets lost. Quarter it or drop it.
- Diluted golden syrup loses the sugar concentration that keeps it shelf-stable. Mix only what is needed, refrigerate, use within a week.
- One pot of 300ml double cream covers both the batter (**75g**) and the whipped topping (**150g**) with a little spare.
