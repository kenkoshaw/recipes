# Golden Diner Pancakes

**Makes** 6 thick pancakes, 3 servings · **Total** 2 hr 30 · **Active** 30 min

Golden Diner's (New York) tall pancakes, by chef Sam Yoo. Yeast and soured milk make them light; they're baked in a hot pan in the oven, then soaked in a honey and soy butter sauce, with a berry topping and lemon zest.

## Method

1. **Make the starter** · 1 hr

   Warm the soured milk jug to 37°C, body temperature. It will look curdled; that's fine.

   Fit the mixer bowl to the Kenwood with the K-beater. Pour in the warm soured milk and mix on low until smooth. Cover and leave at room temperature for 1 hr, until bubbly.

2. **Make the honey butter** · 5 min

   While the starter rises, whisk the honey butter bowl until smooth. If it won't come together, microwave it on low in 20-second bursts until the butter is just soft enough. Leave it at room temperature.

3. **Cook the berries** · 5 min

   Put the berry pan over medium heat, with no water. Simmer gently for 2–3 min, stirring, until the juices thicken. Take off the heat.

4. **Finish the batter** · 5 min

   Heat the oven to 250°C (230°C fan) with an oven-proof non-stick frying pan inside.

   Tip the dry mix bowl into the starter and mix on low with the K-beater until mostly combined. Whisk the egg and oil jug with a fork, add it and mix on low for 10–20 seconds, just until combined. A few lumps are fine.

5. **Bake the pancakes** · 1 hr

   Weigh the batter (about **1530g**). Each pancake takes about **255g**.

   Take the hot pan out with an oven glove and pour in one pancake's worth of batter. Bake for 9 min, until risen and golden. Turn it out onto a plate, golden side up. Keep cooked pancakes warm under foil.

   Put the pan back in the oven for 2 min to reheat, then bake the next one. Repeat for all 6.

6. **Sauce and serve** · 5 min

   Scoop 2 spoonfuls of the honey butter into neat ovals for the tops. Melt the rest in a small pan over low heat, whisking, until it's a smooth sauce.

   Stack 2 pancakes per plate and pour the sauce over. Top with the berries and an oval of honey butter. Finish each plate with lemon zest (1 tbsp, **~6g**).

## Tips

- Treat the hot pan handle with care: use an oven glove or a folded tea towel every time.
- Stovetop instead of the oven: heat the pan over medium-high, then turn it to low. Cook each pancake for about 3 min until bubbles show on top, flip, then cook 1–2 min more. These pancakes are thick, so check the middle is cooked.
- The berry topping is optional; fresh or frozen berries both work.
- The pancakes are filling: one each is plenty for most people.
- Mix the batter only briefly once the bicarb and eggs go in. Overmixing makes the pancakes tough.
