# Gazpacho

**Makes** 4 servings · **Total** 2 hr 15 min · **Active** 15 min

Classic Andalusian cold tomato soup. Cucumber in the blend plus fresh on top, red pepper for sweetness. Best made the day before.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped | 1000 |
| Red pepper, deseeded and roughly chopped | 150 |
| Cucumber, peeled (150 blended + 50 garnish) | 200 |
| Onion | ½ small (60g) |
| Garlic cloves | 10 |
| Day-old white bread, crusts removed | 60 |
| Extra virgin olive oil | 55 |
| Sherry vinegar | 30 |
| Cold water | 150 |
| Salt | 5 |
| Sugar (optional, to balance acidity) | 2 |

### Bowl A — blender vegetables (step 2)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped | 1000 |
| Red pepper, deseeded and roughly chopped | 150 |
| Cucumber, peeled (blended portion) | 150 |
| Onion | ½ small (60g) |
| Garlic cloves | 10 |

### Bowl B — vinegar, salt and sugar (step 3)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Sherry vinegar | 30 |
| Salt | 5 |
| Sugar (optional, to balance acidity) | 2 |

## Method

1. **Soak the bread** · 5 min

   Tear the bread (**60g**) into pieces and submerge in the cold water (**150g**). Leave for 5 minutes until fully soft and waterlogged.

2. **Blend** · 3 min

   Keep the other **50g** of the peeled cucumber (**200g** in all) aside for the garnish. Add Bowl A (tomatoes **1000g**, red pepper **150g**, cucumber **150g**, ½ small onion **60g** and garlic **10g**) to a blender along with the soaked bread and all its soaking water. Blitz on high for 2 minutes until very smooth.

3. **Emulsify** · 2 min

   With the blender running on medium, drizzle in the olive oil (**55g**) slowly to emulsify. Add Bowl B (sherry vinegar **30g**, salt **5g**, and sugar **2g** if needed). Blitz a further 30 seconds.

4. **Strain** · 5 min

   Pass through a fine mesh sieve into a bowl, pushing through with the back of a ladle. Discard the solids.

5. **Chill** · 2 hr minimum

   Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight. Flavour improves significantly with time.

6. **Serve** · 2 min

   Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve ice cold with a drizzle of olive oil and the reserved cucumber (**50g**), finely chopped, on top. Optional extras: finely diced tomato, croutons, or basil leaves.

## Tips

- Tomatoes are everything — use the ripest available. Out of season, supplement with a handful of sun-dried tomatoes.
- Sherry vinegar is traditional; worth using over red wine vinegar.
- Straining is optional but gives a noticeably silkier result.
- A small dried chilli blended in adds a good background heat.
