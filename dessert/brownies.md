# Brownies

**Makes** 24 brownies (23×33cm tin) · **Total** ~1 hr 30 + cooling · **Active** ~30 min

Tasty's "Ultimate Brownies" — melted-chocolate-plus-cocoa fudgy style. The 10-minute egg beat and the mid-bake tray drop are the two steps that make it, don't shortcut them.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Unsalted butter, plus more softened for greasing | 283 |
| Flaky sea salt, for sprinkling | 2 |

### Chocolate bowl (step 2)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Semisweet/bittersweet chocolate (60–70% cacao), roughly chopped | 227 |
| Dutch process cocoa powder (for the chocolate) | 21 |

### Egg and sugar bowl (step 3)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Granulated sugar | 400 |
| Dark brown sugar, packed | 110 |
| Vanilla extract | 2 tsp (9g) |
| Sea salt | 2 tsp (12g) |
| Large eggs | 6 (330g) |

### Dry mix bowl (step 5)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Plain (all-purpose) flour | 125 |
| Dutch process cocoa powder (for the dry mix) | 43 |

## Method

1. **Prep the tin** · 5 min

   Rack in the middle, oven to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan. Grease a 23×33cm metal tin with softened butter, line with baking paper leaving overhang on all sides, grease the paper too.

2. **Hot butter over chocolate** · 8 min

   Heat the butter (**283g**) in a small saucepan over medium until vigorously simmering, about 5 minutes, swirling occasionally. Pour the hot butter over the chocolate bowl and leave for 2 minutes, then whisk until completely smooth. Set aside.

3. **Beat the eggs and sugars** · 10 min

   Beat the egg and sugar bowl with an electric mixer on high for a full 10 minutes — light, fluffy, thick pancake-batter consistency. This is what gives the tender fudgy texture and shiny top.

4. **Combine** · 2 min

   With the mixer running, pour in the chocolate-butter mixture and blend until smooth.

5. **Fold in the dry** · 3 min

   Sift in the dry mix bowl. Fold gently with a spatula until just combined — no beating.

6. **First bake** · 20 min

   Pour into the tin, smooth the top. Bake 20 minutes until lightly puffed.

7. **Drop and salt** · 2 min

   Take the tin out and drop it flat onto the worktop once or twice to deflate slightly. Sprinkle with the flaky sea salt (**2g**).

8. **Second bake** · 20 min

   Back in for 20 minutes more. Done when a skewer in the centre comes out fudgy but the edges look fully cooked — it keeps setting as it cools, slightly underbaked-looking is correct.

9. **Cool completely** · 1 hr+

   In the tin on a rack, fully cool. Lift out by the paper overhang, cut into 24 bars.

## Tips

- Quality chocolate and Dutch process cocoa carry the flavour — don't substitute natural cocoa.
- The full 10-minute beat at step 3 is non-negotiable for the texture.
- Sift the dry ingredients — avoids lumps and overworking the batter.
- The mid-bake drop evens the texture and prevents dense, flat brownies.
- Original includes espresso powder (1 tbsp, ~7g) into the chocolate bowl at step 2 — omitted here; add back for deeper chocolate flavour, doesn't taste of coffee.
- Original specifies a dark metal pan at 180°C; glass or light tins may need a few minutes longer.
