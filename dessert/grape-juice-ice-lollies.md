# Grape Juice Ice Lollies

**Makes** 2–3 small lollies · **Total** 5 hr · **Active** 10 min

Pure juice alone freezes rock-hard and icy. Honey lowers the freezing point for a lolly you can bite, salt and lemon push the flavour back through the cold.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Welch's 100% grape juice | 185 |
| Lemon juice | ½ tsp (3g) |

### Honey and salt bowl (step 1)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Honey | 7 |
| Salt | 0.3 |

## Method

1. **Dissolve the honey** · 2 min

   Put a mug on the scale and zero it, then pour in about a fifth of the grape juice (**35g** of the **185g**). Warm it for 20–30 seconds in the microwave. Stir in the honey and salt bowl until fully dissolved and no grit remains on the bottom. Honey will not disperse in cold juice — it sinks and freezes into a lump at the base of the lolly.

2. **Combine and season** · 3 min

   Tip the warm mixture back into the remaining grape juice (**150g**) and stir well. Add ½ tsp (**3g**) lemon juice and taste at room temperature. It should read slightly too sweet and slightly too tart — freezing mutes both. Add up to **2g** more lemon if it tastes flat, but leave the salt alone; at this volume it is easy to overshoot and impossible to undo.

3. **Chill** · 30 min

   Fridge the mix before it goes near the moulds. A cold mix freezes faster and forms smaller ice crystals, which is the difference between smooth and shattery.

4. **Fill the moulds** · 2 min

   Pour in, leaving roughly 1cm of headroom in each mould — the juice expands as it freezes and will push the lids off. If the moulds do not hold the sticks upright, freeze for 45–60 minutes first, then push the sticks in once the mix is slushy.

5. **Freeze** · 4–6 hr

   Freeze until solid all the way through. Test by pressing the centre of the top — no give at all means done.

6. **Unmould** · 1 min

   Run the mould under warm tap water for 10–20 seconds, then pull the stick with one steady motion. Rocking or twisting snaps the stick out of the lolly.

## Tips

- Taste the mix at room temperature, not fridge-cold — cold blunts sweet and sour, so a mix that tastes balanced warm will taste thin frozen.
- Cornflour is an alternative to honey for texture: **2g** cooked into a splash of the juice, cooled, then stirred back in. Softer body, no added sweetness.
- Grape juice stains badly. Eat outside or over a plate.
