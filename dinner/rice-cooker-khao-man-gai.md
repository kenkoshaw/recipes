# Rice Cooker Khao Man Gai

**Makes** 2 servings · **Total** 1 hr 5 · **Active** 10 min

カオマンガイ — Hainanese-style chicken rice from Cookpad: the chicken cooks on top of seasoned rice in the rice cooker, served with a soy, oyster and miso sauce. No fish sauce by default; add it to the sauce for a more Thai taste.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Japanese short-grain rice, washed and drained | 2 rice-cooker cups (300g) |
| Water | ~370ml (370g) |
| Boneless skin-on chicken thigh, pierced all over with a fork | 1 large (~300g) |
| Spring onion green tops, or the green top of 1 leek | ~25 |

### Rice seasoning bowl (step 1)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Chicken stock cube, crumbled | ½ (5g) |
| Garlic, grated | ½ tsp (3g) |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 1 tsp (5g) |
| Salt | pinch (1g) |
| Black pepper | pinch |

### Sauce bowl (step 4)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Spring onion white parts, finely chopped | 15 |
| Fresh ginger, grated | ½ tsp (3g) |
| Soy sauce | 2 tsp (12g) |
| Oyster sauce | 2 tsp (12g) |
| Caster sugar | 1 tsp (4g) |
| Miso | 1 tsp (6g) |
| Lemon juice | 1 tsp (5g) |
| Water | 1 tbsp (15g) |
| Sesame oil | ½ tsp (2g) |
| Fish sauce (optional) | 1 tsp (6g) |

## Method

1. **Season the rice** · 2 min

   Put the rice (**300g**) in the rice cooker's inner pot and tip in the rice seasoning bowl. Zero the scale with the pot on it and add water until it reads **370g**: a little under the 2-cup line, since the chicken releases juice as it cooks.

   Stir well so the stock cube dissolves and the garlic and ginger spread through.

2. **Chicken on top, start the cooker** · 50 min

   Lay the spring onion greens (**25g**) on the rice, then the chicken (**300g**) on top, skin side down. Don't stir. Start the normal white rice programme.

3. **Check and slice the chicken** · 5 min

   Lift out the chicken and throw away the onion greens. The thickest part should read 75°C or more; if not, put it back on the rice and keep warm for 10 min. Slice into 1.5cm strips.

   Fluff the rice with a paddle so the chicken juices mix through.

4. **Serve** · 2 min

   Pile the rice into bowls, lay the chicken on top and stir the sauce bowl, then spoon it over or serve it on the side.

## Tips

- **Less water than usual**: water stays a little under the 2-cup line because the chicken releases juice. Cookpad cooks who used the full line got soft rice.
- **Sides**: the source serves it with sliced cucumber and tomato, plus coriander if you like it.
- **Sauce ahead**: the sauce bowl keeps a day in the fridge and tastes better after the spring onion has softened in it.
