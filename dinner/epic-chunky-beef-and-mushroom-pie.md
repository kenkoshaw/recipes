# Epic Chunky Beef and Mushroom Pie

**Makes** 6 servings · **Total** 2 hr 45 · **Active** ~45 min

Tender chunks of beef and mushrooms in a rich gravy, under a puff pastry lid. Dried porcini give the gravy a savoury depth. The filling is better made the day before.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Dried porcini mushrooms | 20 |
| Boiled water, for the porcini | 312ml (312g) |
| Chuck beef, cut into 2.5cm cubes | 1000 |
| Vegetable oil | 1–2 tbsp (~14–28g) |
| Salt and pepper, to season the beef | to taste |
| Carrots, halved lengthwise and cut into 1.5cm chunks | 3 (~300g) |
| Flour (any) | ~42 |
| Bacon, diced | 200 |
| Swiss brown mushrooms, larger ones halved | 300–400 |
| Puff pastry, ready-rolled, enough to cover the pie | 1 sheet (~320g) |
| Egg yolk | 1 (~18g) |

### Aromatics bowl (step 4)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Garlic cloves, minced | 2 (~6g) |
| Onion, finely chopped | 1 (~150g) |

### Carrot and celery bowl (step 4)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Carrot, finely chopped | 1 (~100g) |
| Celery, finely chopped | 1 stalk (~50g) |

### Ale and stock jug (step 5)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Dark ale (or red wine, Guinness or more beef stock) | 300ml (300g) |
| Beef stock | 500ml (500g) |

### Herb bowl (step 6)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried) | 3 sprigs (~3g) |
| Bay leaves | 2 (~1g) |

## Method

1. **Soak the porcini** · 30 min

   Put the dried porcini (**20g**) in a bowl, pour in 312ml (**312g**) boiled water and leave for at least 30 min.

2. **Strain the porcini** · 5 min

   Strain the soaked porcini and keep the soaking liquid (**312g**). Roughly chop the porcini (**20g**) and add them to the herb bowl.

3. **Brown the beef** · 10 min

   Sprinkle the beef (**1000g**) with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp (**~14g**) oil in a large heavy-based pot.

   Brown half the beef all over, remove it, then repeat with the rest. Set the beef aside.

4. **Soften the vegetables** · 12 min

   Turn the heat down to medium-low. If the pot looks dry, add a little more oil.

   Tip in the aromatics bowl and cook for 2 min. Add the carrot and celery bowl and cook for 6 min, until softened and sweet.

   Add the 3 carrots (**~300g**) and cook for 2 min.

5. **Flour and liquid** · 3 min

   Add the flour (**~42g**) and stir it through. Pour in the ale and stock jug and stir to dissolve the flour.

6. **Simmer the stew** · 1 hr 45

   Add the herb bowl with the chopped porcini (**20g**), the porcini soaking liquid (**312g**, leaving the gritty bits at the bottom of the bowl) and the browned beef (**1000g**).

   Turn the heat up slightly, stir, bring to a simmer and cover. Adjust the heat so it simmers gently, not energetically.

   Cook for 1 hr 45, or until the beef is tender.

7. **Bacon and mushrooms** · 15 min (while the stew simmers)

   Cook the bacon (**200g**) in a skillet over high heat until golden. Take it out and keep the drippings in the pan.

   Add the mushrooms (**300–400g**) and cook for 5 min, until golden all over. Return the bacon to the skillet, toss to coat the mushrooms, then stir it all through the stew.

8. **Finish the stew** · 15 min

   Simmer the stew uncovered for 15 min, until the mushrooms are tender. Take it off the heat and let it cool. Take the puff pastry out of the fridge.

9. **Assemble the pie** · 10 min

   Heat the oven to 200°C. Pour the cooled stew into a pie dish and even out the surface.

   Cover with the puff pastry sheet (**~320g**), folding the ends in if it's too big. Brush with 1 egg yolk (**~18g**) and cut a few slits in the top.

10. **Bake** · 30–35 min

    Bake until deep golden and the filling is piping hot in the middle. Stick a knife in to check.

11. **Rest** · 5 min

    Rest for 5 min before serving.

## Tips

- **Porcini**: they add a savoury earthiness to the gravy. If you can't get them, it's still worth making, or try other dried mushrooms (from the source).
- **Beef**: any slow-cooking cut works. Chuck and gravy beef are the source's favourites, and brisket is also good. Avoid lean cuts like bolar blade roast, which dry out (from the source).
- **Pie dish**: the source used a 24×20×4.5cm dish (1.5L). The filling should come up to the top, or just below the rim, so the pastry sits nicely. A 20cm square pan or a deep 23cm pie dish also works, and so do individual ramekins.
- **Slow cooker**: skip the stock and use 2 crumbled beef stock cubes. Follow the method up to step 6, then slow cook for 6 hours on low or 3 hours on high. Add the bacon and mushrooms and cook for another 45 min. For a pressure cooker, pressure cook on high for 25 min instead (from the source).
- **Make ahead**: the filling tastes even better the next day. Cool it overnight, then top with pastry and keep it in the fridge for up to 2 days. The filling freezes well, with or without the pastry on top. Thaw before baking and brush with egg just before it goes in (from the source).
- **Kenwood**: no step here uses the mixer.
