# Miso-Yoghurt Chicken Thighs with Crème Fraîche Miso Pasta

**Makes** 4 servings · **Total** ~5 hr (4 hr marinade) · **Active** ~40 min

Chicken thighs marinated in Greek yoghurt, brown miso, honey and garlic, pan-fried in stainless steel until charred, then sliced over a quick crème fraîche pasta made in the same pan. The yoghurt and crème fraîche share a tang that ties the dish together, and the fond from the chicken gives the sauce its depth. It also works with short grain rice and roasted purple sweet potato instead of pasta.

| Ingredient | Grams |

## Method

1. **Marinate** · 4 hr–overnight

   Whisk the marinade bowl until smooth. Coat the chicken (**600g**) in it.

   Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight, and no more than 24 hours.

2. **Bring the chicken out** · 20 min

   Take the chicken out 20 minutes before cooking. Scrape each side with the back of a knife until only a thin, see-through film of marinade remains. Bin the leftover marinade.

3. **Pasta water on** · 10 min

   Put a large pan of water on to boil and salt it normally.

4. **Heat the pan** · 4 min

   Preheat a stainless steel pan on medium for 3–4 minutes, then add 1 tbsp (**14g**) oil.

5. **Fry the chicken** · 12 min + 5 min rest

   Lay the thighs smooth side down and leave them for 5–6 minutes, until a crust forms and they release. Flip and cook for another 5–6 minutes, until they reach 75°C inside.

   If they darken too fast, slide the pan off the heat for 30 seconds. Rest on a board for 5 minutes.

6. **Cook the pasta** · ~10 min

   Cook the pasta (**320–400g**) to 1 minute short of the packet time. Scoop out a mugful of pasta water before draining.

7. **Start the sauce in the chicken pan** · 2 min

   Over low heat, melt the butter (**15g**) into the fond and soften 4 cloves (**20g**) garlic for 1 minute.

   Deglaze with a splash of pasta water and scrape up the browned bits.

8. **Miso and crème fraîche** · 2 min

   Whisk the brown miso (**10g**) into a little pasta water until smooth and add it to the pan. Stir in the crème fraîche (**300g**).

9. **Combine** · 2 min

   Toss in the pasta and Parmigiano (**60g**), loosening with pasta water until glossy. Taste before adding any salt.

10. **Serve** · 2 min

    Slice the chicken and lay it on top rather than stirring it in. Scatter over the garnish bowl.

## Tips

- **Thin coat only.** Thick marinade steams instead of browning, and the honey and miso burn before the chicken is cooked.
- **Never reuse the marinade.** That covers sauce, glaze and another batch of raw chicken. Boiling it to make it safe splits the yoghurt. For more flavour, use the cooked pan fond or tray juices, or mix a fresh batch.
- **Electric hob.** It responds slowly, so move the pan off the heat rather than turning it down.
- **Full-fat crème fraîche** can be simmered. Half-fat can split, so stir it in off the heat.
- **Salt stacks up.** The miso, soy and Parmigiano are all salty, so add no extra salt until the end.
- **White miso swap.** Use **25g** white miso, **15g** soy, **21g** honey and **15g** rice vinegar instead.
- **Oven alternative.** Bake at 200°C fan for 18–22 minutes, then grill for 1–2 minutes, watching closely.
- **Rice alternative.** Serve with short grain rice and purple sweet potato cubes roasted at 200°C for 25–30 minutes.
- **Freezing.** Freeze the chicken raw in the marinade, flat in a zip bag. Thaw it in the fridge (it marinates while it thaws), cook within 24 hours of thawing, and don't refreeze it raw.
- **Leftovers.** Refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking. Reheat at 180°C for 8–10 minutes until steaming hot all the way through.
