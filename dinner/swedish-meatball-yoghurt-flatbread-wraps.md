# Swedish Meatball Yoghurt Flatbread Wraps

**Makes** 6 wraps · **Total** 35 min · **Active** 30 min

Frozen Swedish meatballs in quick Greek yoghurt flatbreads, with dill yoghurt sauce, lingonberry and gherkins. For a slower yeast-free flatbread, see [Easy Soft Flatbread](../bread/easy-soft-flatbread.md).

## Method

1. **Mix the dough** · 3 min

   Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Fit the mixer bowl to the Kenwood with the dough hook, tip in the dough yoghurt bowl and mix on low for 1 min, then medium-low for 1 min, until a soft dough forms.

   Work quickly from here: the bicarb starts reacting as soon as the yoghurt goes in. If the dough is very sticky, add dusting flour (**~20g**) 1 tbsp at a time.

2. **Divide and roll** · 5 min

   Weigh the dough (about **600g**) and divide it into 6 pieces of about **100g**. Roll each out on a floured surface to a 20cm round, 2–3mm thick.

3. **Bake the meatballs** · 20 min

   Spread the frozen meatballs (**500g**) over the roasting tray and bake for 20 min, or as the pack says, until piping hot and 75°C in the middle. Carry on with the flatbreads while they bake.

4. **Cook the flatbreads** · 18 min

   Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat with no oil. Cook each flatbread for 1½ min a side, until puffed with golden spots.

   Stack them wrapped in a clean tea towel so they stay soft.

5. **Assemble** · 5 min

   Spread each flatbread with the dill yoghurt bowl. Top with the lettuce (**~100g** in all), 5 meatballs, 1 tbsp (**15g**) lingonberry sauce and some gherkins (**~60g** in all). Roll up and serve.

## Tips

- The yoghurt and lemon juice are the acid for the bicarb, so use full-fat Greek or Greek-style yoghurt, not a thin or sweetened one.
- If the flatbreads crack when rolled, brush them with a little olive oil or melted butter and keep them in the tea towel.
