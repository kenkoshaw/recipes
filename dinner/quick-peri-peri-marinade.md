# Quick Peri Peri Marinade

**Makes** enough for 1kg chicken or veg · **Total** 1 hr 10 · **Active** 10 min

Store-cupboard peri peri — smoked paprika and cayenne base, honey to balance. Halve everything for 500g of protein.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Olive oil | 83 |
| Lemon juice | 60 |
| Garlic, minced (6 cloves) | 30 |
| Smoked paprika | 9 |
| Cayenne pepper | 5 |
| Chilli flakes (optional, extra kick) | 3 |
| Dried oregano (or thyme) | 2 |
| Salt | 12 |
| Honey (or sugar) | 14 |
| Ground black pepper | 2 |

## Method

1. **Mix** · 5 min

   Whisk the olive oil (**83g**), lemon juice (**60g**), minced garlic (**30g**), smoked paprika (**9g**), cayenne (**5g**), chilli flakes (**3g**), oregano (**2g**), salt (**12g**), honey (**14g**) and black pepper (**2g**) in a bowl until the honey has fully dissolved.

2. **Marinate** · 1 hr minimum

   Coat 1kg of chicken or veg thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour; overnight is better for chicken.

3. **Cook** · varies

   Grill, roast or BBQ as the cut demands. Discard any marinade that has touched raw chicken — reserve a spoonful before coating if you want some for basting or serving.

## Tips

- Cayenne sets the heat level — drop to ½ for mild, and the chilli flakes are the optional extra kick on top.
- Lemon juice can be swapped for vinegar for a sharper finish.
- Honey over sugar — it dissolves into the oil more readily and helps char.
