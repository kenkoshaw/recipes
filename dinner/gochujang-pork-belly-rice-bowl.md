# Gochujang Pork Belly Rice Bowl

**Makes** 2 servings · **Total** ~40 min · **Active** ~15 min

Sticky, spicy-sweet jeyuk-style pork belly over short-grain rice. The pork marinates while the rice cooks.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Short-grain (sushi) rice, rinsed until the water runs mostly clear | 250 |
| Water (for the rice) | ~325 |
| Skinless pork belly, sliced ~5mm thick | 400 |
| Spring onions, cut into 4cm lengths | 3 (~45g) |
| Toasted sesame seeds | 1 tsp (~3g) |
| Eggs (optional, for frying) | 2 (~100g) |

### Marinade bowl (step 2)

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|
| Gochujang | 2 tbsp (~40g) |
| Gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes) | 1 tbsp (~6g) |
| Soy sauce | 2 tbsp (~36g) |
| Sugar | 1 tbsp (12g) |
| Mirin or honey | 1 tbsp (~20g) |
| Toasted sesame oil | 1 tbsp (13g) |
| Garlic, grated | 3 cloves (~12g) |
| Fresh ginger, grated | 1 tsp (~5g) |
| Black pepper | ¼ tsp (~0.5g) |
| Onion, sliced | 1 (~150g) |

## Method

1. **Cook the rice** · 25 min

   Put the rice (**250g**) and water (**~325g**) in a pan. Bring to a boil, put the lid on and cook on low heat for 12 min.

   Take it off the heat and rest it, lid still on, for 10 min.

2. **Marinate the pork** · 20 min–overnight

   While the rice cooks, mix the marinade bowl well. Add the pork (**400g**) and toss until every slice is coated.

   Leave for 20 min, or overnight in the fridge.

3. **Render** · 3 min

   Heat a large pan over medium-high heat with no extra oil. Add the pork and onion from the marinade bowl in a single layer, in batches if crowded.

   Leave undisturbed for 2–3 min, until it releases from the pan.

4. **Glaze** · 6 min

   Stir-fry for 5–6 min until cooked through and sticky. Lower the heat if it starts to burn.

   Add the spring onions (3, **~45g**) for the last minute.

5. **Serve** · 5 min

   If using, fry the eggs (2, **~100g**). Spoon the rice into two bowls and the pork over it. Top with sesame seeds (1 tsp, **~3g**) and a fried egg.

## Tips

- **Stainless pan**: get it properly hot first, don't move the pork until it releases, and deglaze with a splash of water if it scorches.
- **Heat level**: adjust with gochugaru, not gochujang.
- **No gochugaru**: swap in chipotle chilli flakes at about half the amount, 1½ tsp (**~3g**); it's smokier than gochugaru. For a closer match, use ¾ tsp (**~1.5g**) chipotle with 2 tsp (**~4g**) sweet paprika.
- **Sides**: kimchi, quick-pickled cucumber, or lettuce leaves for ssam-style wraps.
- **Kenwood**: no step here uses the mixer.
