# Swedish Meatballs

**Makes** 6 servings · **Total** ~1 hr 45 · **Active** ~45 min

Butter-basted beef and pork meatballs in a cream gravy, with pomme purée, shop-bought lingonberry (or cranberry) sauce and pickled cucumber. The meatballs chill for an hour, which is when the potatoes get done.

| Ingredient | Grams |
|------------|-------|

| Pork mince | 500 |

## Method

1. **Soak the breadcrumbs** · 5 min

   Fit the K-beater and mix the mixer bowl on minimum speed for a few seconds to combine. Leave it to sit for 5 min.

2. **Mix the meatballs** · 2 min

   Add the beef mince (**500g**), pork mince (**500g**) and the spice bowl. Mix on minimum speed with the K-beater for about 1 min, until just combined. Stop as soon as it looks even; overmixing makes them tough.

3. **Shape and chill** · 1 hr

   With wet hands, roll into golf-ball-sized meatballs (about **40g** each, roughly 30). Put them on a tray and chill in the fridge for 1 hr.

4. **Boil the potatoes** · 35 min (while the meatballs chill)

   Put the potatoes (**~1000g**) in a pan of cold water, bring to the boil and cook for about 35 min, until a knife slides in with no resistance.

   Drain. Peel them while warm, then push them through a sieve back into the dry pan. Set aside off the heat.

5. **Sear the meatballs** · 10 min

   Heat the olive oil 2 tbsp (**~27g**) in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Sear the meatballs in batches until browned all over.

6. **Butter baste** · 5–8 min

   Return all the meatballs to the pan and turn the heat to medium. Add the basting bowl.

   Tilt the pan and spoon the foaming butter over the meatballs until cooked through, 71°C in the middle. Take the meatballs out and leave the pan as it is.

7. **Gravy** · 15 min

   In the same pan, pour in the stock and cream jug and scrape up the browned bits. Pick out the herb stalks. Simmer until reduced by about half and it coats a spoon, 10–12 min.

   Whisk in the Dijon mustard 1 tsp (**~5g**). Return the meatballs and warm them in the gravy for 3–5 min.

8. **Finish the purée** · 10 min (while the gravy reduces)

   Put the pan of sieved potatoes over low heat. Fold in the cold butter (**~227g**) a few cubes at a time, until creamy.

   Pour in the milk (**~245g**), add the salt 1–2 tsp (**6–12g**) and fold gently. Serve with the meatballs and gravy, the lingonberry sauce (**~200g**) and the pickled cucumber (**~300g**).

## Tips

- **Lingonberry sauce**: IKEA's lingonberry jam is the classic. Otherwise use any cranberry sauce; whole-berry beats jelly.
- **Kenwood**: K-beater on minimum for the meatball mix, and stop as soon as it's combined. Fold the purée by hand; beating cooked potato in the mixer makes it gluey.
- **Pickles**: pick a sweet-sour cucumber pickle, not sharp gherkins in malt vinegar. Good options are Polish "ogórki konserwowe" (not "kiszone", which are sour), Mrs Elswood Sweet & Sour Cucumbers sliced thin, or bread and butter pickles with some fresh dill.
- **Homemade sauce and pickles**: the source cooks lingonberries or cranberries (**~200g**) with caster sugar (**~67g**) and lemon juice 1 tbsp (**~15g**) until jammy, without squashing the berries. For the pickles it dissolves caster sugar (**~150g**) in white wine vinegar (**~480g**), water (**240g**) and mustard seeds 1 tbsp (**~10g**), then adds 1 sliced cucumber (**~300g**) and dill (**~5g**).
